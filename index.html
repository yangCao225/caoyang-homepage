Selected Research Projects

Robust Environmental Intelligence via Digital Twin and AI

First Author  |  Research Project

Developed a Digital Twin-based decision-support framework for urban air quality monitoring under sensor failures. Proposed a Mask-Aware Spatio-Temporal Model (MAST-AQ) integrating observation masks, graph-based spatial learning, and temporal modeling to enhance prediction robustness under missing monitoring stations.

  • Built a fault-aware cyber-physical monitoring framework for urban environmental systems.
  • Designed robustness evaluation protocols under simulated station outages.
  • Achieved reliable PM2.5 prediction under up to 50% station failure scenarios.

Learning When Not to Bind: Reliable Multimodal Learning under Semantic Mismatch

First Author  |  Research Paper

Investigated the reliability issue of vision-language models under cross-modal semantic mismatch. Proposed an Independent Admission Gate mechanism that decouples feature relevance estimation from binding decisions, enabling models to selectively reject unreliable multimodal fusion.

  • Introduced a magnitude-admission decomposition framework for selective multimodal binding.
  • Developed internal rejection signals for mismatch detection and uncertainty estimation.
  • Improved model reliability while preserving clean-sample retrieval performance.

Surrogate-Assisted Constrained Multi-Objective Optimization with Scarce Data

First Author  |  CCF Conference Submission

Studied multi-objective optimization problems under limited experimental data and expensive evaluations. Developed a surrogate-assisted optimization framework combining Gaussian Process modeling and evolutionary Pareto search.

  • Designed a data-efficient optimization pipeline for constrained black-box problems.
  • Integrated surrogate modeling with multi-objective evolutionary optimization.
  • Enabled efficient decision exploration with limited observations.

Solar-Powered Electrochemical Nitrogen Removal for Smart Agriculture

Research Member  |  Undergraduate Innovation Project

Designed a low-carbon electrochemical treatment system for agricultural wastewater remediation by integrating solar energy supply, pulse electrolysis, and NiFe-LDH catalytic electrodes.

  • Developed a solar-driven nitrate removal prototype.
  • Evaluated energy efficiency and carbon reduction potential.
  • Explored sustainable solutions for smart agriculture and environmental management.
