About

I am an undergraduate researcher at Xiangtan University working on trustworthy artificial intelligence, with interests in robust machine learning, multimodal reliability, and AI-enabled environmental systems. My research aims to build intelligent systems that remain reliable under uncertainty — whether from missing sensor observations, semantic mismatches between modalities, or scarce experimental data.

Selected Research

Robust Environmental Intelligence via Digital Twin and AI

First Author  |  Research Project

How can AI systems remain reliable when environmental observations become incomplete?

Proposed MAST-AQ, a mask-aware spatio-temporal model integrating observation masks, graph-based spatial learning, and temporal modeling. Built a fault-aware cyber-physical monitoring framework and achieved robust PM2.5 prediction under up to 50% station failure scenarios.

Learning When Not to Bind: Reliable Multimodal Learning

First Author  |  Research Paper

How should vision-language models decide when to refuse fusion rather than force it?

Proposed an Independent Admission Gate mechanism that decouples feature relevance estimation from binding decisions. Developed internal rejection signals for mismatch detection, improving model reliability while preserving clean-sample performance.

Data-Efficient Multi-Objective Optimization under Scarce Data

First Author  |  CCF Conference Submission

How can we optimize complex systems when experiments are expensive and data is scarce?

Developed a surrogate-assisted optimization framework combining Gaussian Process modeling with evolutionary Pareto search, enabling efficient decision exploration with limited observations.
